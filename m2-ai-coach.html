Most programmes are generic. Your development shouldn't be.

Players and fitness clients often receive programmes that ignore their age, level, position, available equipment, weekly schedule and previous training.

The M2 AI Coach is being built to understand the person first, then create the plan.

Random online workouts
Drills that don't match the player's position
Exercises requiring equipment you don't own
Programmes that are too difficult or too easy
No clear progression
No connection between football and gym training
No adaptation when goals or circumstances change

The Coach brings assessment, programme design, exercise demonstrations and progression into one connected system.

How It Works

1

Tell The Coach about you

Complete an intelligent profile covering:

  • Age and football position
  • Ability and goals
  • Main weaknesses
  • Current training schedule
  • Gym access and available equipment
  • Preferred session duration
  • Injury and safety information
2

The Coach builds your plan

The system filters the M2 exercise library based on your profile.

  • Age appropriateness
  • Playing position and goals
  • Available equipment and environment
  • Session length and training demands
  • Solo or partner availability

The Coach may only select exercises from the approved M2 library.

3

Follow guided sessions

Each session includes:

  • Exercise videos
  • Setup instructions
  • Coaching points
  • Common mistakes
  • Progressions and regressions
  • Session structure
  • Completion tracking
4

Review and progress

The platform will use check-ins and training feedback to support future programme updates.

  • Session completion tracking
  • Difficulty and confidence feedback
  • Pain or soreness monitoring
  • Changing goals and available time
  • Performance development

Built from real coaching, not random internet workouts

The platform is based on:

  • Marco's football coaching experience
  • Strength and conditioning knowledge
  • Work with youth, grassroots and high-level players
  • Real exercise demonstrations
  • Structured player assessments

Built by Marco Muthi

Marco is a football coach, personal trainer and performance coach with a background in Sport and Exercise Science and Performance Football Coaching. The M2 AI Coach is being created to turn the M2 coaching system into a platform players can access wherever they train.

Personalised does not mean uncontrolled

  • Exercises come from the approved M2 library
  • The AI cannot freely invent exercises
  • Age and equipment rules are applied
  • Exercise safety data is human-reviewed
  • Player injury information can be recorded
  • Unclear or serious safety cases may require further review
  • The platform does not provide medical diagnoses
Always follow guidance from your doctor, physiotherapist or qualified medical professional where applicable.
